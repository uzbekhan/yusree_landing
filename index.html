Ask us anything
01Why wait for Yusree when free debt apps already exist?

Because most “free” debt apps are paid for by lenders. They show you a balance, then sell you a loan, a new card or a consolidation offer. Yusree is built to do one thing only: get you to zero. No loan promotion, no card pushing, no affiliate offers. Ever.

02How fast can a payoff plan make me debt-free?

Yusree turns every balance into a dated finish line. Compare Snowball, Avalanche, Balanced or your own Custom order side by side, and see in seconds how a small extra payment moves your debt-free date and cuts total interest.

03Is Yusree a lender, bank or card company?

No — and it never will be. Yusree is an ethical, educational debt management tool. It helps you organise, plan and pay down what you already owe; it never lends, brokers or pushes financial products. Its own shop services — like the Yusree card and gift vouchers — exist only to help you pay down and give, never to borrow.

04Who sees my financial data?

Only you. Sharing personal details is off by default and requires your explicit consent. Publicly, only your chosen nickname can ever appear — and only if you switch it on.

05Does Yusree support zakat, hardship and my language?

Yes. Yusree includes zakat and hardship guidance, any world currency, and works in 10 languages including Arabic and Urdu with full right-to-left support — on any phone, installable from the browser, no app store needed.

06What will it cost at launch?

The core debt payoff planner is free. No trial that turns into a bill, no data sold to pay for it.
